· how it works ·
01

setup

Install Nibble from the Chrome Web Store and connect your Gmail account. Takes less than a minute.

→
02

star

In Gmail, star the newsletter emails you want to read. Don't worry about any other starred items. Nibble will hunt for relevant content.

→
03

nibble

Every new tab, one article from your starred list. It finds you. You don't have to go looking.

04

nibble, daily, watch it bloom

every day you nibble, your garden gets one bloom fuller.
