what it does

Too many newsletters, not enough time? Nibble helps you work through the backlog one article at a time, with no pressure.

  1. Star the newsletters you want to read in Gmail, as they arrive.
  2. Open a new tab. Nibble picks one starred newsletter for the day.
  3. Click Nibble to read it. Come back tomorrow for the next one.

extras

  • Reading garden: read daily and your garden grows. Skip a day and it wilts.
  • Shortcuts and recent tabs: your most-visited sites and recently closed tabs, right on the page.
  • Six themes to pick from.
  • Won't repeat the same sender too often.

your data

Nibble has no server. It reads the headers and a short snippet of your starred emails (read-only, never the full message) and keeps everything in your browser. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else. Read the full privacy policy.
