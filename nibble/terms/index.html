What Nibble does

Nibble replaces your new tab page. If you choose to connect your Google account, it reads the headers and a short snippet of emails you have starred in Gmail (read-only) and shows you one of them each day. It also shows shortcuts and recently closed tabs. How your data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
