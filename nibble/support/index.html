Nibble Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Email nechalmaggon@gmail.com and include what you saw and which version of Chrome you use. I read every message.

Common questions

I see "connect gmail to start"

Click Connect Gmail and approve the permission. Nibble only asks for read-only access to message details (sender, subject, date and a short snippet) and never the full email. If you close the popup, the button stays so you can try again.

Nibble says "all nibbled up"

You've already been shown every starred newsletter Nibble has found. Star more newsletters in Gmail and open a new tab.

My starred email isn't showing up

Nibble only shows emails that have an unsubscribe header (the usual sign of a newsletter) and skips automated senders such as no-reply addresses, notifications and receipts. It also avoids repeating the same sender too often.

How do I disconnect Nibble from Gmail?

Remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Nibble stores is on your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

I installed Nibble from this website, not the Chrome Web Store

That version needs your own Google OAuth client ID. Open a new tab and click the setup guide link, or open setup.html from the extension, for step-by-step instructions.
