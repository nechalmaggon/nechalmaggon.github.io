lessons that turned into rules

let people opt into complexity

not everyone needs every feature, and clutter slows people down. fewer clicks isn't always better.

keeping it simple

enterprise software gets complicated fast, and users end up carrying the load. if it's hard to explain without docs, it needs a rethink.

defaults do the heavy lifting

most people never touch settings. the default is the product.
