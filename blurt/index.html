00:01 · SOUNDS LIKE YOU?

Your brain doesn't do one thing at a time.

It's a grocery list, then a genuinely good idea for work, then suddenly you're thinking about that email you never replied to — all in the same breath, all equally urgent, all gone in about ninety seconds if you don't catch it.

Most apps want you to stop and deal with that. Open the app. Pick a folder. Name the note something sensible. By the time you've done all that, the thought already left.

Blurt skips that part.

00:02 · OKAY BUT HOW
STEP 01

Talk

Hit record wherever you are — walking, driving, mid-scroll, doesn't matter. No menu to dig through first. Just talk like you'd talk to a person who already gets it.

STEP 02

Blurt catches up

It figures out whether you were planning something, venting, or having a genuinely great idea, and quietly files it — as a to-do, an idea, or just a note — without you doing a thing.

STEP 03

It doesn't just sit there

A to-do gets started on. An idea gets looked into. You come back later to something further along than where you left it, not just tidier.
